# Schools, Gender Transition & Parental Consent

Oct 24, 2026 · @Zach Jusice

## Bottom line

The concern has two parts, and the evidence treats them very differently.

**Social transition without parental notice: real, documented, and now largely settled in parents' favor.** Some districts had written policies letting staff use a student's new name and pronouns at school without telling parents unless the student agreed. Several lawsuits involve families who say this happened to their child. In March 2026 the Supreme Court sided with parents in the leading California case, though that ruling was preliminary, not final. ([AP](https://abc7news.com/post/supreme-court-blocks-law-schools-outing-transgender-students-parents-california/18669175/))

**Medical or surgical transition by schools: false.** Except in emergencies, schools do not provide medical care without parental consent, and gender-affirming surgery on minors is rare. ([PolitiFact](https://www.politifact.com/factchecks/2024/sep/05/donald-trump/trumps-pants-on-fire-claim-that-schools-are-perfor)) When CNN asked the Trump campaign for an example of a school sending a child for surgery without parental consent, it could not provide one. Parental consent is also required for minors to receive puberty blockers or hormones. ([CNN](https://abc17news.com/politics/national-politics/cnn-us-politics/2024/09/04/fact-check-trump-falsely-claims-schools-are-secretly-sending-children-for-gender-affirming-surgeries/))

A conservative parents' group leader told CNN that schools are not doing surgeries, while still thanking Trump for highlighting schools that facilitate social transitions without parental consent. That is a fair summary of where the real dispute lies.

## Timeline

The issue began with a handful of 2020–21 school cases, became a national fight over California's 2024 law, and reached the Supreme Court in March 2026. Listed oldest first to show how it entered the mainstream.

| When | Event | Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2020–21 | **Leon County, Florida (Littlejohn):** a 13-year-old at Deerlake Middle School asked to use a male name and they/them pronouns; parents sued in 2021. ([Orlando Weekly](https://www.orlandoweekly.com/news/court-refuses-to-reconsider-case-of-florida-student-who-wanted-to-use-pronouns-their-parents-didnt-support-39997172/)) | School won 2–1 at the 11th Circuit (Mar 2025); Supreme Court declined the case Apr 27, 2026. ([SCOTUSblog](https://www.scotusblog.com/cases/littlejohn-v-school-board-of-leon-county/)) |
| 2020–21 | **Ludlow, Massachusetts (Foote):** an 11-year-old asked for a new name and pronouns; the counselor told staff to use the birth name with parents. ([Bay Windows](https://www.baywindows.com/story/153366)) | 1st Circuit ruled for the district Feb 18, 2025; Supreme Court denied review Apr 2026, no noted dissents. ([Reason](https://reason.com/volokh/2026/04/20/after-mirabelli-the-other-foote-did-not-drop/printer/)) |
| 2021–24 | **Eau Claire, Wisconsin:** policy allowed name, pronoun and bathroom changes without parental notice; district said plans were in student files. ([Reuters](https://979weve.com/?p=648724)) | Dismissed for lack of standing; Supreme Court declined Dec 2024, one vote short, Alito dissenting. ([CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/chicago/news/wisconsin-scotus-ruling-transgender-students-school-guidance/)) |
| Aug 28, 2023 | **Chino Valley, California:** state Attorney General sued the district over its parental notification policy. ([Ballotpedia](https://news.ballotpedia.org/2024/09/05/responses-to-california-bill-banning-gender-identity-parental-notification-policies-ab-1955/)) | District rescinded the policy in March 2024 after an injunction. ([Signal](https://signalscv.com/2024/07/scv-leaders-react-to-new-law-banning-parent-notification-of-student-gender-identity)) |
| Jul 15, 2024 | **California AB 1955 signed:** first state to ban parental notification policies, effective Jan 1, 2025. ([EdSource](https://edsource.org/2024/newsom-signs-bill-to-end-parental-notification-policies-at-schools-but-opponents-say-its-not-over-yet/715767)) | Chino Valley and parents sued the next day. |
| Aug 30, 2024 | **Surgery claim:** Trump said schools push students into surgery without parental consent. | Rated "Pants on Fire" by PolitiFact and false by CNN. |
| Jun 18, 2025 | ***U.S. v. Skrmetti:*** Supreme Court upheld Tennessee's ban on gender-affirming medical care for minors, 6–3. ([KFF](https://www.kff.org/policy-watch/what-are-the-implications-of-the-skrmetti-ruling-for-minors-access-to-gender-affirming-care/)) | 27 states had such bans as of June 2025. |
| Mar 2, 2026 | ***Mirabelli v. Bonta:*** Supreme Court reinstated an order blocking California's policies for parents while the case continues; Kagan and Jackson dissented. ([CSBA](https://blog.csba.org/mirabelli-march26/)) | California argued students have a privacy right, especially if they fear rejection at home. |
| Jun 18, 2026 | **9th Circuit** blocked key parts of AB 1955 for seven parents, relying on *Mirabelli*. ([Daily Journal](https://dailyjournal.com/article/392394-9th-circuit-blocks-parts-of-california-school-gender-identity-law)) | California's appeal of the underlying ruling continues. |
| Sep 2026 | **DOJ sued Kansas City, Kansas schools** over helping students socially transition without parents' knowledge. ([The Advocate](https://www.advocate.com/politics/national/doj-sues-kansas-transgender-students)) | District calls the suit baseless. |
| Oct 2026 | **Chatham, New Jersey** district agreed to permanently drop its policy, settling a Moms for Liberty suit. ([NJ Globe](https://newjerseyglobe.com/local/chathams-school-board-settles-lawsuit-over-transgender-student-notification-policy/)) | Board approved 7–0. |
