A note from me

Hey Dad! We talk a bunch about politics, and I've thought deeply about the best way to communicate how I see these issues. Part of me believes that if I put together the right set of objective, reliable sources from trustworthy organizations, then you might see my point of view.

These are evidence reviews on questions we have talked about. Each one includes direct links to articles, reports, court records, and other source material, plus notes about where the evidence is strong and where it is limited.

If you ever find yourself saying “That's no way that can be true” or “This news outlet/article/source is biased,” each article includes a link to continue exploring the original research conversation.
