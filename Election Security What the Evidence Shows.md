# Election Security

Oct 24, 2026 · @Zach Jusice

Reviews by Republican officials, GOP-led audits, conservative groups and news outlets have found that voter fraud occurs and is prosecuted, but at levels far too small to change statewide results. Sources below favor Republican officials, conservative organizations and Fox-affiliated outlets where available.

## 2020 election reviews

Every review below was conducted or commissioned by Republicans, and each upheld the certified result.

## Fraud found vs. votes cast

- **AP review of six battleground states (Dec. 2021):** Reporters contacted about 300 local election offices and found fewer than 475 potential fraud cases, about 0.15% of Biden's combined margin. Cases involved voters of both parties. [Fox 13 / AP](https://www.fox13news.com/news/far-too-little-vote-fraud-to-tip-2020-election-to-trump-ap-review-finds)
- **Heritage Foundation Election Fraud Database:** Passed 1,500 proven cases in 2024, up from 1,071 when it launched in 2017; 98 cases were added in 2023. Heritage describes it as a sampling, not a complete count, meant to show vulnerabilities. For scale, U.S. general elections draw well over 100 million ballots each cycle. [Daily Signal](https://www.dailysignal.com/2024/01/29/heritages-one-kind-election-fraud-database-hits-1500-cases/)

## What the evidence does and doesn't show

- **Shows:** Fraud and illegal registrations occur, are usually caught, and are prosecuted. The numbers found are small relative to votes cast and to winning margins.
- **Shows:** Citizenship-check tools can over-flag. In Texas, about 1 in 5 flagged registrants were later confirmed as citizens.
- **Does not settle:** Policy debates such as proof-of-citizenship requirements or mail voting rules. Supporters cite documented cases and vulnerabilities; opponents cite the low rates and the risk of removing eligible voters.
