# Trump Administration Ethics Concerns: Reading List

Oct 24, 2026 · @Zach Jusice

## Overview

This list collects reporting on conflict-of-interest concerns in Trump's second term, from wire services, major newspapers and conservative outlets. Most of the conduct described is legal; the debate is over whether it is appropriate. Allegations that remain unproven are marked as such.

- [How the Trump family's business deals could open the door for future presidents to profit from office](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/how-the-trump-familys-business-deals-could-open-the-door-for-future-presidents-to-profit-from-office) (AP via PBS, Apr 13, 2026). The Trump Organization did no foreign deals in the first term and is now expanding overseas faster than at any point in its history.
- Wall Street Journal editorial board (2026). The Murdoch-owned board criticized the family for profiting from the presidency in ways that demean the office. Summary via [The Daily Beast](https://www.thedailybeast.com/murdoch-paper-the-wall-street-journal-eviscerates-donald-trump-after-bombshell-financial-disclosure); the original is on WSJ.com.

## Crypto ventures

The family's crypto businesses have drawn foreign money while the president shapes U.S. crypto policy.

- **UAE stake in World Liberty Financial.** Days before the 2025 inauguration, the family sold nearly half of the company for $500 million to a UAE government-linked firm run by a member of the royal family. First reported by the Wall Street Journal; covered in the AP piece above.
- [Trump meme coin buyers vie for dinner with the president. Many appear to be overseas.](https://cbsnews.com/news/trump-meme-coin-dinner-foreign-top-bidders) (CBS News). More than half the top holders competing for a November dinner used exchanges that bar U.S. users.
- [Trump draws global crypto investors to golf club dinner](https://www.aol.com/trump-draws-global-crypto-investors-120551820.html) (Reuters via AOL, May 2025). Buyers spent an estimated $148 million on the coin to win seats; Republican Sen. Cynthia Lummis said the event gave her pause.

## Stock trades

Trump's 2026 disclosures show thousands of individual stock trades, including in companies that deal with his administration.

- [Trump's more than 3,700 trades in three months](https://www.newstribune.com/news/2026/may/18/trumps-more-than-3700-trades-in-three-months/) (AP, May 18, 2026). Trades in Q1 2026 involved major companies doing business with the administration. Trump is the first president to trigger the STOCK Act disclosure rule.
- [What Donald Trump knows about his stock investments](https://www.notus.org/money/donald-trump-stock-investments-portfolio-disclosure) (NOTUS). Explains who manages the trades and reports that Trump filed late under the STOCK Act, paying a $200 fine.

## Trump's sons and federal contracts

Fifteen companies backed by Donald Trump Jr. and Eric Trump have won $3.2 billion in government business since the brothers invested, plus $3.1 billion in future options.

- [Trump's sons invest heavily in defense tech as father's administration pours money in](https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2026/07/13/trumps-sons-invest-heavily-in-defense-tech-as-fathers-administration-pours-money-in/) (Washington Post, reprinted by Anchorage Daily News, Jul 13, 2026). The Post notes 10 of the 15 companies already had government business before the investments, and 8 had contracts under Biden.

## Jared Kushner

Kushner faces a lawsuit over his envoy role and, separately, unproven allegations from a new book.

- [Watchdog group sues Trump administration over Jared Kushner's government work](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/watchdog-group-sues-trump-administration-jared-kushners-government-wor-rcna603563) (NBC News, Oct 14, 2026). CREW argues Kushner serves as envoy without Senate confirmation or financial disclosures while running a $6 billion firm funded about 99% by foreign investors, including Saudi, Qatari and UAE sovereign funds.
- [Kushner denies Vanity Fair allegation of undisclosed bank account](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/oct/23/jared-kushner-denies-vanity-fair-allegation-undisclosed-bank-account/) (Washington Times, Oct 23, 2026). A book excerpt alleges Kushner was a signatory on a UAE account that received about $750 million. **Unverified:** Kushner calls the claims fabricated and plans legal action. See also [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/kushner-rebukes-completely-insane-report-223718611.html).

## The administration's response

The White House denies any conflict of interest.

- Trump's assets are in a trust managed by his children, and he has no involvement in family business deals, per the White House (AP).
- Presidents are exempt from the federal conflict-of-interest statute that covers other officials, so much of this activity is legal.
- The sons say they have no role in awarding or overseeing government contracts, and several of their companies won contracts before they invested.
