Election Security

Oct 24, 2026 · @Zach Jusice

Reviews by Republican officials, GOP-led audits, conservative groups and news outlets have found that voter fraud occurs and is prosecuted, but at levels far too small to change statewide results. Sources below favor Republican officials, conservative organizations and Fox-affiliated outlets where available.

2020 election reviews

Every review below was conducted or commissioned by Republicans, and each upheld the certified result.

Review Who conducted it Finding
DOJ statement (Dec. 2020) Attorney General Bill Barr, Trump administration No fraud found on a scale that could have changed the outcome
"Lost, Not Stolen" (July 2022) Conservative judges, lawyers and former GOP senators, incl. Ted Olson and Ben Ginsberg Reviewed all 64 cases in six states; no evidence fraud changed the result in any precinct (Fox News coverage)
Michigan Senate Oversight Committee (June 2021) GOP-led state Senate committee No widespread or systemic fraud; results accurately represented
Arizona "Cyber Ninjas" audit (Sept. 2021) Contractor hired by GOP-led Arizona Senate Hand count confirmed Biden won Maricopa County, by 360 more votes than the official count
Wisconsin Legislative Audit Bureau (Oct. 2021) Nonpartisan auditors, ordered by GOP-controlled Legislature No widespread fraud; dozens of recommendations to improve procedures

Fraud found vs. votes cast

  • AP review of six battleground states (Dec. 2021): Reporters contacted about 300 local election offices and found fewer than 475 potential fraud cases, about 0.15% of Biden's combined margin. Cases involved voters of both parties. Fox 13 / AP
  • Heritage Foundation Election Fraud Database: Passed 1,500 proven cases in 2024, up from 1,071 when it launched in 2017; 98 cases were added in 2023. Heritage describes it as a sampling, not a complete count, meant to show vulnerabilities. For scale, U.S. general elections draw well over 100 million ballots each cycle. Daily Signal

Noncitizen voting checks

State reviews, most led by Republicans, have found noncitizen registration and voting in small numbers.

State review Led by Registered voters checked Result
Maricopa County, AZ (Sept. 2026) Republican county recorder and county attorney County rolls 207 suspected noncitizen registrants referred; 8 confirmed to have voted in elections from 2012 to 2025
Georgia, DHS claim (July 2026) DHS; response from Republican Sec. of State Raffensperger 8.1 million DHS cited about 2,500; the state says most were applicants held in pending status, not registered voters
Texas (Oct. 2025) Republican-appointed Sec. of State Jane Nelson 18 million+ 2,724 flagged as possible noncitizens; 506 later proven citizens after passport data was added
Utah (Jan. 2026) Republican Lt. Gov. Deidre Henderson 2 million+ 1 noncitizen found; never voted
Georgia (Oct. 2024) Republican Sec. of State Raffensperger 8.2 million 20 noncitizens found; 9 had voted in past elections

Voting machine claims

In April 2023, Fox News paid Dominion Voting Systems $787.5 million to settle a defamation suit over 2020 broadcasts. Before trial, the judge ruled that all 20 statements cited in the suit were false. Fox said it acknowledged the court's rulings that certain claims about Dominion were false. Axios · Lawyer Monthly

What the evidence does and doesn't show

  • Shows: Fraud and illegal registrations occur, are usually caught, and are prosecuted. The numbers found are small relative to votes cast and to winning margins.
  • Shows: Citizenship-check tools can over-flag. In Texas, about 1 in 5 flagged registrants were later confirmed as citizens.
  • Does not settle: Policy debates such as proof-of-citizenship requirements or mail voting rules. Supporters cite documented cases and vulnerabilities; opponents cite the low rates and the risk of removing eligible voters.
