# Fox News Credibility: A Source Guide

Oct 24, 2026 · @Zach Jusice

Fox News's prime-time opinion programming has a documented record of airing claims its own hosts privately doubted, and courts, settlements and former Fox insiders confirm it. Sources below are ordered by how hard they are for a Fox viewer to dismiss: Fox's own words and court findings first, then conservative insiders, then outside research.

## The court record

A Delaware judge ruled Fox's Dominion statements false as a matter of law, and Fox paid $787.5 million rather than go to trial.

The Smartmatic case ties directly to the Venezuela framing: Fox aired claims linking a voting company to Venezuela's socialist government that the company says were knowingly false.

## On air vs. off air: January 6

Three Fox hosts privately begged the White House to stop the riot, then downplayed it on air.

- **Private:** Ingraham texted Mark Meadows that Trump needed to tell people to go home. Kilmeade asked him to get Trump on TV. Hannity asked whether Trump could make a statement. Republican Rep. Liz Cheney read the texts aloud. ([AP](https://whyy.org/articles/fox-stars-tried-to-get-trump-to-act-on-jan-6-texts-show))
- **On air:** Hours after the attack, Ingraham promoted the false claim that antifa was involved, and Fox hosts often minimized Jan. 6 in the months after. ([NYT via Star-Advertiser](https://www.staradvertiser.com/?p=1074579))
- **Coverage choices:** Fox did not carry Cheney's hearing live, and Hannity interviewed Meadows without asking about the texts. ([AP](https://whyy.org/articles/fox-stars-tried-to-get-trump-to-act-on-jan-6-texts-show))

## What Fox leaves out

Regular Fox viewers paid to watch CNN for one month in September 2020 shifted their views and became more likely to say Fox hides bad news about Trump.

- **Design:** Researchers at Yale and UC Berkeley randomly assigned 304 of 763 regular Fox viewers to watch CNN instead. ([Yale News](https://news.yale.edu/2022/04/13/partisan-media-cable-viewers-shift-attitudes-after-changing-channel))
- **Coverage gap:** CNN devoted 10,251 words to COVID severity that month; Fox devoted 709. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2022/04/12/fox-cnn-news-media-diet-partisan-media-coverage-study-partisan-coverage-filtering/amp))
- **Result:** Switchers became significantly more likely to disagree that Fox would report something bad Trump did. The authors call this "partisan coverage filtering": slanting by choosing what to cover.

## Why outrage sells

Anger toward the other side is the strongest known driver of engagement, which gives every partisan outlet, left or right, a business reason to provoke it.

| Finding | Detail | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook's own 2018 slide | "Our algorithms exploit the human brain's attraction to divisiveness." Executives reportedly shelved fixes. Reported by the Murdoch-owned WSJ. | [Coverage of WSJ report](https://www.neowin.net/amp/facebook-reportedly-knew-its-algorithms-were-dividing-people-yet-did-nothing/) |
| Facebook's angry-emoji weighting | From 2017, reactions counted 5x a like. Facebook's data scientists found angry-reaction posts were disproportionately misinformation; Fox News drew nearly double the angry reactions of any other page. | [Washington Post via ADN](https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2021/10/26/five-points-for-anger-one-for-a-like-how-facebooks-formula-fostered-rage-and-misinformation/), [Nieman Lab](https://niemanlab.org/?p=197146) |
| Rathje et al., PNAS 2021 | Across 2.7M posts, each word about the political out-group raised the odds of a share by 67%, regardless of political side. | [PNAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/8256037) |
| Brady et al., PNAS 2017 | Each moral-emotional word in a tweet raised its spread by 20%. | [PNAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5514704) |
| Rasmussen et al., 2025 | In randomized headline tests, negativity about out-groups raised clicks 14.1%, versus 1.6% for negativity about one's own group. | [SSRC](https://mediawell.ssrc.org/?p=511831) |

## How to raise it

Research suggests that attacking someone's channel or flooding them with the other side's media often backfires; respectful, specific conversation works better.

- **Separate news from opinion.** Nearly every insider critic praises Fox's hard-news reporters. "Trust the reporting, not the prime-time hosts" is more accurate and less threatening than "Fox is all lies." ([WRAL/CNN](https://www.wral.com/archive/17431022/))
- **Avoid forced exposure.** Republicans paid to follow a liberal Twitter bot for a month became more conservative. ([Bail et al., PNAS 2018](https://scholars.duke.edu/publication/1346854))
- **Address the perception gap.** Partisans on both sides think nearly twice as many opponents hold extreme views as actually do. ([More in Common](https://moreincommon.substack.com/p/what-we-get-wrong-about-each-other))
- **Listen and trade stories.** Conversations using arguments alone changed nothing; adding a non-judgmental exchange of personal stories produced changes lasting four months. ([Kalla & Broockman, APSR 2020](https://isps.yale.edu/node/24743))
- **Answer his specific reasons.** Tailored, evidence-based dialogue cut conspiracy beliefs about 20% for at least two months. ([Costello et al., Science 2024](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/node/48296))

## Counterpoints

Expect these responses; some have merit.

- **Context:** Fox argued its Dominion statements were taken out of context, and settled without admitting liability. ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2023/03/31/1167526374/judge-rules-fox-hosts-claims-about-dominion-were-false-says-trial-can-proceed))
- **Jan. 6 texts:** Hannity and Ingraham said their texts matched what they said on air, and Hannity did condemn the violence. ([AP](https://whyy.org/articles/fox-stars-tried-to-get-trump-to-act-on-jan-6-texts-show))
- **Stirewalt:** Murdoch said his departure had nothing to do with the Arizona call. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2022/06/10/fired-fox-news-editor-chris-stirewalt-to-testify-at-jan-6-riot-committee-hearing.html))
- **Peters:** He was never a Trump supporter; Fox called his letter an attention-seeking move. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2018/03/21/fox-news-analyst-quits-rips-network-as-a-propaganda-machine-in-letter-to-colleagues.html))
- **Facebook data:** Every emoji, not just "angry," got 5x weight at launch, and "angry" was the least-used reaction. Facebook said the WSJ ignored its efforts. ([Cato](https://www.cato.org/blog/washington-post-facebook-promotes-outrage-isnt-outrageous))
- **CNN study:** The effects faded within two months once participants went back to Fox, and the study ran only in one direction. ([CUNY News Literacy](https://newsliteracymatters.commons.gc.cuny.edu/2022/09/19/q-can-changing-your-media-diet-change-your-attitudes/))
- **Both sides:** Some conservatives argue CNN and MSNBC are equally partisan. The outrage research supports that the incentive applies to all outlets.
